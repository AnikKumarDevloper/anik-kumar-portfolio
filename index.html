ANIK KUMAR / INDIA

I'm Anik Kumar, founder of Tube Pilot and an independent product builder.

My work and experiments span Flutter and Dart mobile apps, React-based websites, and AI-powered workflows. I use AI tools to explore ideas, prototype faster and support implementation while continuing to strengthen my development skills.

I like practical ideas, clear interfaces and products that solve real problems. My process is simple: understand the need, shape the experience, build a version, learn from it and improve.
